## How to use an “awesome” list correctly

```text
Community list
     ↓
Candidate source
     ↓
Original publisher
     ↓
Publication date / version
     ↓
Evidence and methodology
     ↓
Cross-source corroboration
     ↓
Analyst assessment
```

Do not cite “awesome list X says Y” when the list merely links to the report that actually says Y. Humans invented hyperlinks specifically so we would not have to do this, yet here we are.

## Curator checks

Before adding a community collection:

- Is it still maintained?
- Are dead links removed?
- Does it identify original sources?
- Is commercial content labeled?
- Does it mix offensive and defensive resources without context?
- Does it distinguish historical projects from active ones?
- Does it disclose ownership or sponsorship?
- Does it provide enough metadata for an analyst to judge relevance?
