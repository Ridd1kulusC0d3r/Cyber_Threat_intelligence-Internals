A minimal regional watchlist for CTI analysts operating in Brazil or Latin America:

1. [CERT.br](https://cert.br/) — Internet incident statistics, abuse handling and technical material.
2. [CTIR Gov](https://www.gov.br/ctir/pt-br) — Brazilian federal-government cyber coordination and alerts.
3. [CAIS/RNP](https://www.rnp.br/sistema-rnp/cais) — Academic/research-network security.
4. [ESET WeLiveSecurity](https://www.welivesecurity.com/) — Frequent LATAM-focused research.
5. [Kaspersky Securelist](https://securelist.com/) — Global and regional threat research.
6. [Group-IB](https://www.group-ib.com/blog/) — Cybercrime and financially motivated threat research.

## Practical reading order for a new report

When a new incident or campaign appears:

```text
1. Primary advisory / affected organization
2. Government or CERT advisory
3. Original vendor research
4. ATT&CK / MISP / Malpedia normalization
5. IOC enrichment across independent sources
6. Secondary reporting for additional leads
7. Internal telemetry and business context
8. Analyst assessment + confidence
```

This order reduces the charming human tendency to cite the tenth repost of a claim instead of the source that actually observed it.
